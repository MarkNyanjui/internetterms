Web 4.0, also known as the Symbiotic Web, is a futuristic concept of the internet where humans and machines interact seamlessly in a highly intelligent and interconnected environment.

Key features

  • Advanced AI integration
  • Ubiquitous connectivity
  • Enhanced human-machine collaboration

Potential impact

Web 4.0 has the potential to revolutionize how we interact with technology, enabling more intuitive and personalized online experiences while fostering deeper connections between humans and machines.
