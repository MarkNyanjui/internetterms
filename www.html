The World Wide Web (WWW) is a global system of interconnected web pages and digital resources accessed through the internet. It was invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989 while he was working at CERN (European Organization for Nuclear Research).

The WWW allows users to access text, images, videos, and applications through hyperlinks. It operates using web browsers and web servers to deliver information worldwide. Users can move from one webpage to another through clickable links, making information retrieval faster and more convenient.

The World Wide Web is often confused with the internet, but the two are different. The internet refers to the global network infrastructure that connects millions of computers worldwide, while the World Wide Web is a service that operates on top of the internet.

  • Provides access to websites and online services
  • Uses hyperlinks for navigation between pages
  • Supports multimedia content such as text, images, audio, and video
  • Enables global communication and information sharing
  • Supports online learning, e-commerce, and social media platforms
